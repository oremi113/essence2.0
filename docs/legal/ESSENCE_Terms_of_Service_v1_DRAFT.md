## 2. What ESSENCE is, and what it is not

**What the Service does today.** ESSENCE lets you record your own voice by reading a series of scripted prompts aloud. We send those recordings to a third-party voice provider, which creates a synthetic model of your voice. You can then create messages inside your account. Each message is generated from a fixed template plus a short personalized note you write, and is rendered as audio in your synthetic voice. You can play back your saved messages inside your account at any time.

**What the Service does not do.** Please read this section carefully, because it is the most important thing to understand about ESSENCE today:

- **We do not deliver messages to anyone.** There is no email, text message, mail, or other delivery of your messages to any other person, at any time, under any circumstance.
- **No one else can listen to your messages.** Messages are played back only by you, from inside your own authenticated account.
- **"Recipients" are private labels, not delivery targets.** When you tag a message with a person's name and relationship, that label is stored privately in your own account. We do not collect that person's email address, phone number, or any other contact information, and we never contact them.
- **There is no scheduled or posthumous release.** ESSENCE has no scheduling feature, no death or incapacity verification, no dead man's switch, no trusted contact, and no release trigger of any kind. Nothing in your account is released to anyone upon your death or at any future date.

If we ever build a delivery feature, we will describe it in updated Terms before it is turned on. **Do not use ESSENCE on the assumption that your messages will reach anyone. They will not.** ESSENCE is, today, a private voice keepsake vault for your own use.

## 6. AI-generated content

Messages in ESSENCE are generated by artificial intelligence. Two things follow from that:

- **The audio is synthetic.** It is a machine-generated approximation of your voice, not a recording of you saying those words. It may contain errors in pronunciation, emphasis, or tone.
- **The words are partly generated.** Your personalized note is rewritten by an AI model into a short passage, and is not spoken back verbatim. The rest of the message is fixed template text. Generated output may not reflect your exact intent. Review a message before saving it.

We make no representation that generated audio is indistinguishable from your real voice, suitable for any particular purpose, or acceptable as proof of identity, consent, or intent. **Never use ESSENCE audio for voice authentication, identity verification, or any other security or legal purpose.**

Messages are immutable once generated. You can discard a message in progress, but a saved message cannot be edited.
