Essence · Breath Stone

A quiet animal that breathes at 3 seconds.

The Breath Stone is Essence's one piece of iconography. It inhabits the moment of recording and the moment of listening. Treat it like a quiet animal — it breathes, it responds, it never performs.

The atmosphere it lives in on ceremonial screens — the six-layer stage, the cream-on-dark ramp, the honey primary — is specified in ds/dark-stage.html.

States

idle
Gentle 3 s breath cycle. Neutral warmth. Pre-tap, between moments.
recording
Pulses with the speaker's voice (RMS-driven in production). Faster cycle, a trace brighter.
priming
Held, charged moment before a ceremonial action — the 3.5 s lock before "Mint my voice".
validation
Warm pulse at completion. Halo brightens in time with breath. Lands. Then rests.
speaking
The stone is voicing the user's own audio. Subsurface core glows from inside; amplitude-driven in production. Speech is not a faster breath — see below.

Clearance & placement

≥ 48 px < 420 px · ≥ 120 px above

Give it room

Clearance is viewport-conditional. ≥ 48 px on every side below 420 px viewport width; ≥ 120 px at 420 px and above. During ceremonial moments the stone is the only moving element on screen.

The hero is fluid: --stone-size-hero: min(320px, 56vw). Other canonical sizes are fixed — 160 px in-card, 220 px default, 120 px minimum rendered (below that it reads as a dot, not a sphere).

Text never sits on top of the stone. If a label belongs nearby, it goes below at 32–48 px separation.

Do · Don't

Do

  • Render as a 2D radial gradient sphere with warm highlights upper-left, warm shadow lower-right.
  • Breathe at 3 s with --ease-breath. Symmetric inhale / exhale.
  • Pearl body on dark backgrounds, honey body on cream backgrounds.
  • Let the halo pulse in time with the stone during validation.
  • Speech differs from breath in character, not amount: a sustained swell across the utterance plus syllable articulation on top. A faster breath reads as agitation, not voice.
  • Drive amplitude states through CSS custom properties on transform and opacity only, from one rAF loop. Never animate box-shadow per frame.
  • Make it quieter when in doubt.

Don't

  • Don't outline it with a hard ring.
  • Don't add sparkle, particles, sheen-sweeps, or secondary ornament.
  • Don't give it a face, eyes, mouth, or limbs.
  • Don't animate faster than the breath cycle — except while the stone is voicing, where articulation follows the cadence of the words.
  • Don't place text on top of it.
  • Don't recolor into electric gradients or neon.
